How it works.

Ambient listens to the hooks each agent already offers. Nothing wraps your agent, and nothing watches your screen.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Open Ambient and click Connect All. It adds its own entries to each agent's hook settings, after backing the file up.

  2. 2

    Listen

    When an agent starts a turn, runs a tool, asks for permission or finishes, its hook sends one short line over a Unix socket on your Mac. In about 15 ms, in the background.

  3. 3

    Show

    Ambient keeps a state for every session and shows the most urgent: needs you, then error, then done, then working.

0 bytes

leave your Mac.

Private by design: no accounts, no network, no telemetry. Events travel over a private socket on your Mac and stay there.

Questions.

Is Ambient free?

Yes. Download it, connect your agents and use every feature.

Is Ambient open source?

Yes. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT license.

Does it send my code or prompts anywhere?

No. Each hook reduces an event to a few short fields — agent, state, project name, a trimmed message — and sends them over a Unix socket to the app on your Mac. The app makes no network requests.

Will it slow my agent down?

No. Hooks finish in about 15 ms, run in the background for Claude Code and Codex, and always exit successfully. If Ambient isn't running, events are simply dropped.

How is Ambient different from Claude Code's built-in notifications?

Claude Code can post a desktop notification in Ghostty, Kitty and iTerm2, or ring the terminal bell elsewhere. Ambient adds a live status for every session of every agent at once, in any terminal or editor. A click takes you back to the exact tab, pane or chat, and Ambient stays quiet while you're already looking at the agent. Compare every option.

Does it work with tmux, iTerm2, VS Code and Cursor?

Yes. Ambient shows the status of an agent in any terminal or editor on your Mac, and in the Claude and Codex desktop apps. A click returns you to the exact tab in Terminal and iTerm2, the exact pane in tmux and cmux, and the project's window in VS Code, Cursor and other editors.

What does it change on my Mac?

It adds its own hook entries to ~/.claude/settings.json, ~/.codex/hooks.json and ~/.gemini/settings.json, keeps a backup of each file, and removes only its own entries when you disconnect an agent or run ambient uninstall.

How do I uninstall Ambient?

Run ambient uninstall to remove Ambient's hooks. Your other settings stay exactly as they were. Then quit Ambient from the menu bar and delete the app and the ~/.ambient folder.

Does the living wallpaper replace my wallpaper?

No. It's off until you turn it on in Settings › Wallpaper, and it's drawn above your own wallpaper and below your desktop icons. Turn it off and your wallpaper is simply there. On the lock screen, Ambient sets the scene as your wallpaper while the Mac is locked, under macOS's own password field and login options, and puts your wallpaper back when you unlock.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. It's designed for MacBooks with a notch; on other displays the island sits at the top center of the menu bar.

Do I need to sign up to download it?

No. The download starts right away. If you want an email when a new version ships, you can leave your address after downloading; that's optional. See the privacy notice.

How do I ask for a feature?

Suggest it on the feature requests board, or vote for someone else's idea there. No account needed. In the app, choose Suggest a Feature… from the menu bar menu.
