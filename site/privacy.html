Privacy

Last updated September 30, 2026.

The app

Ambient collects nothing. It has no accounts, makes no network requests and sends no telemetry. Agent events travel over a private Unix socket on your Mac and stay there.

Download counts

We count downloads so we know how many people try Ambient and which launch posts they came from. Each download adds one anonymous record: the date, the version, the ref tag of the link you followed (or the site that linked to the download) and your country. It holds no IP address, no cookie and nothing that identifies you.

Website analytics

To see which pages help people and which links bring them here, this site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service. It sets no cookies, doesn't store your IP address and doesn't follow you across sites. It records the pages you view, the site or ref tag that sent you, your browser, operating system, device type and country, clicks on the download and update buttons, how quickly pages load and respond (Core Web Vitals), and how the living wallpaper demo is used and how smoothly it runs. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is recorded.

Feature requests

The feature requests board is public: what you write there, and how many votes it has, anyone can see. Posting and voting need no account. Your browser keeps a random ID so you vote once per request; we store only a scrambled version of it. To limit spam, we count posts and votes per network each day using a one-way hash whose secret changes every day, so it can't be traced back to your IP address or linked across days. We don't store IP addresses, cookies or email addresses for the board. To have a request removed, email us.

Update emails

Downloading Ambient doesn't ask for anything. After the download starts you can leave your email address to hear when a new version ships. We use it only to tell you about Ambient — new versions and new features. We don't sell or share it. If you arrived from a link with a ref tag, such as one in a launch post, we store that tag with your address so we know which posts people found useful.

The form is stored with Netlify, which hosts this site. Netlify may log standard request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the site.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, email vivek.yadav@stringswaytech.com or reply to any email from us, and we'll do it. Every email we send includes a way to stop receiving them.
